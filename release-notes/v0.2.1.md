**Improved**
- Pixel-art cursors stay sharp on Retina displays and when you resize a theme again after saving it.
- Importing long cursor animations uses less memory while preserving their overall playback timing.

**Fixed**
- The overlay keeps the macOS cursor visible when it cannot identify the current cursor or draw its replacement.
- You can still restore the standard macOS cursors after a theme switch is interrupted.

Apple Silicon only. Curstyle is not notarized: on first launch, choose **Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
